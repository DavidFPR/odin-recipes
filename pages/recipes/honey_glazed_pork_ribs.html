Honey Glazed Pork Ribs

Ingredients

  • 2 lbs pork ribs
  • 1/2 cup honey
  • 1/4 cup soy sauce
  • 1/4 cup apple cider vinegar
  • 2 tbsp Dijon mustard
  • 2 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 tsp ground black pepper
  • 1 tsp paprika
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp ground ginger
  • 1 tbsp vegetable oil

Directions

  1. Preheat the oven to 160°C (325°F).
  2. In a bowl, whisk together honey, soy sauce, apple cider vinegar, Dijon mustard, garlic, black pepper, paprika, salt, and ground ginger.
  3. Place the pork ribs on a baking sheet lined with aluminum foil. Brush both sides of the ribs with the honey glaze mixture.
  4. Cover the ribs with foil and bake in the preheated oven for 2.5 to 3 hours, or until the meat is tender and starts to pull away from the bones.
  5. Remove the foil and brush the ribs with additional glaze. Increase the oven temperature to 220°C (425°F) and bake for an additional 15-20 minutes, or until the glaze is caramelized and slightly crispy.
  6. Let the ribs rest for a few minutes before slicing and serving.

A Little Bit of History...

Honey glazed pork ribs are a modern twist on ancient cooking techniques that combine sweet and savory flavors. The use of honey as a glaze has historical roots in many cultures, including those of the Vikings. Although honey was not commonly used for cooking in Viking times, the tradition of sweetening meat with natural ingredients has been around for centuries. In the Viking era, preserving and flavoring meats was crucial for survival, and methods like smoking or salting were commonly employed.

The concept of glazing meat with a sweet substance likely evolved over time, influenced by trade and exploration. As spices and sweeteners became more available, recipes began to incorporate these ingredients to enhance flavors. Honey, in particular, was valued for its sweetness and preservative qualities, making it a popular choice for glazes and sauces. The combination of honey with savory elements like soy sauce and mustard reflects a fusion of traditional and contemporary culinary practices.

Today, honey glazed pork ribs are enjoyed for their rich, caramelized flavor and tender texture. They serve as a reminder of how ancient ingredients and techniques continue to influence modern cooking. Whether prepared for a feast or a casual meal, these ribs bring a taste of history to the table, celebrating the enduring appeal of sweet and savory combinations in cuisine.
