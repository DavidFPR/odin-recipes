Viking Flatbread (Lefse)

Ingredients

  • 2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 1 cup mashed potatoes (cooled)
  • 1/4 cup butter, softened
  • 1/2 cup milk
  • 1/2 tsp salt
  • 1/2 tsp baking powder

Directions

  1. In a large bowl, mix the flour, salt, and baking powder.
  2. Add the mashed potatoes and butter, mixing until crumbly.
  3. Gradually add the milk and mix until a dough forms.
  4. Turn the dough out onto a floured surface and knead gently until smooth.
  5. Divide the dough into small balls and roll each out into a thin circle.
  6. Heat a griddle or skillet over medium-high heat. Cook each flatbread for 1-2 minutes per side, until lightly browned and cooked through.
  7. Serve warm with butter, cheese, or your favorite toppings!

A Little Bit of History...

Lefse, a traditional Viking flatbread, is a beloved staple in Scandinavian cuisine. Dating back to the Viking Age, this flatbread was a practical and versatile food that could be easily transported and stored. Made from simple ingredients like flour and potatoes, lefse was a common item in the Viking diet, providing a nutritious base for various toppings.

The preparation of lefse reflects the Vikings' resourcefulness and adaptability. Potatoes, which became a staple in later Viking periods, were combined with flour to create a flatbread that could be baked on a griddle. This method of cooking was ideal for the Viking lifestyle, allowing them to prepare a hearty and portable food that could be eaten on the go or preserved for later.

Lefse continues to be a cherished part of Scandinavian heritage. It is often enjoyed during festive occasions and is a symbol of traditional Nordic cuisine. The simple yet flavorful flatbread connects modern-day eaters with the Viking past, embodying the essence of Viking culinary traditions and their enduring influence on Scandinavian food culture.
